JobZone

A full-stack job board connecting employers and job seekers through two dedicated portals. Job seekers build a profile with qualifications, experience, and CV upload; employers create a company profile and post vacancies with structured requirements and experience-level filtering. An admin panel oversees the platform, with location, nationality, and industry taxonomies powering search and matching underneath.

Client: JobZone (Portfolio / Case Study)
Type: Full-Stack Development — Python, Flask, MySQL
Year: 2026
Three-role architecture — job seeker, employer, admin — each with its own routes, forms, and permissions, sharing one relational schema underneath.
